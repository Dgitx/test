7 technical SEO fixes that unlock sustainable growth

A practical framework for finding and fixing the issues that stop good content from ranking.

RK
Riley KimGrowth Strategy · October 2026

Technical SEO is most valuable when it removes friction between your strongest content and the people searching for it.

1. Start with crawlability

Before optimizing individual pages, make sure search engines can reliably discover, crawl and understand the parts of the site that matter.

2. Fix performance bottlenecks

Prioritize issues that affect real user experience: oversized media, render-blocking scripts and unstable layouts.

Good technical SEO makes everything else easier to compound.

3. Build useful internal links

Internal linking clarifies hierarchy, distributes authority and helps users continue their journey.
